Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (version 2.0.0)

Effective date: November 2026

1. Introduction

StorageNinja ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles information when you use it. StorageNinja is a browser extension that helps developers and testers manage the browser storage of the page they are on: Local Storage, Session Storage, Cookies, IndexedDB and Cache Storage.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data We Do NOT Collect

StorageNinja does NOT collect, sell or transmit any of the following to us or to any third party:

  • Personal information or identifiable data
  • Your browsing history or website visits
  • Storage data from your browser (Local Storage, Session Storage, Cookies, IndexedDB, Cache Storage)
  • Authentication information such as access tokens or session cookies
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device information or system details

2.2 Data Handled Locally

To do its job, StorageNinja reads and changes data that websites keep in your browser. This data can include authentication information (for example access tokens and session cookies) and website content. It is handled only on your device, only for the feature you use, and is never sent to a server:

  • Viewing, editing, copying and pasting storage data of the current page, and detecting the page's access token so you can copy it
  • Clipboard: data is placed on or read from your clipboard only when you choose copy or paste
  • Snapshots: when you save a snapshot, the site's Local Storage, Session Storage and cookies are stored in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device until you delete the snapshot or uninstall the extension
  • Settings: your theme and the token source you pinned per site are stored locally in the same way

3. How We Use Information

Since we don't collect any information, we don't use, share, or sell any personal data. StorageNinja operates as a purely local tool that:

  • Accesses a page's storage only when you open the extension, its DevTools panel, its context menu or its keyboard shortcut
  • Processes data locally within your browser environment
  • Keeps data between sessions only for snapshots and settings you create
  • Does not communicate with external servers and loads no remote code

4. Permissions Explained

StorageNinja requests the following Chrome permissions:

Permissions:

  • Host access to all sites (<all_urls>): Chrome's cookies API only works for sites the extension has host access to; temporary activeTab access is not enough for cookies. StorageNinja uses it only for the page you are using it on.
  • cookies: Read, edit and delete the cookies of the current site
  • scripting: Run small functions in the current page to read and change its Local Storage, Session Storage, IndexedDB and Cache Storage
  • activeTab: Access the tab you invoke the extension on
  • contextMenus: The StorageNinja entry in the page's right-click menu
  • browsingData: "Clear all & reload" for the current site only
  • clipboardRead / clipboardWrite: Copy and paste when you choose to
  • offscreen: Use the clipboard from the context menu and keyboard shortcut
  • storage / unlimitedStorage: Keep your snapshots and settings on your device

These permissions are used solely for the extension's user-facing features and never for data collection, advertising or tracking.

5. Data Security

Since all data processing occurs locally on your device:

  • Your data never leaves your browser environment
  • No network transmission of sensitive information occurs
  • Storage data remains under your complete control
  • The extension cannot access data from other extensions or browser components

6. Third-Party Services

StorageNinja does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or external APIs. The extension operates entirely offline and independently.

7. Children's Privacy

Our extension does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data collection occurs, COPPA compliance concerns do not apply to our service.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last modified" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
